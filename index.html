ABOUT US

A jeweller who comes to you, and charges fairly.

Hehank Gems & Jewellery was built on a simple belief: beautiful jewellery shouldn't cost you a showroom's markup. Big brands add up to 28% in making charges. We work at 0–7%, and put the difference back into the piece.

You bring the design, from a photo, a sketch, or a piece you saw elsewhere. We meet you where you're comfortable, understand exactly what you want, and craft it with the same care, often better.
